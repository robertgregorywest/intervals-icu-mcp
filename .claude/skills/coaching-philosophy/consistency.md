# Consistency

Progress compounds from showing up. Frequency over heroics — five short rides beat one weekend
blowout. Missing one session is noise; missing weeks is signal.

Define a **minimum viable week** you can hit even when life pushes back, and have a plan B (turbo,
shorter, strength) ready _before_ you need it.

> _e.g._ Minimum viable week: 3×45-min rides + 1 strength session, sustainable through travel and
> busy work blocks.
