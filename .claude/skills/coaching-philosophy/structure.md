# Structure

Sessions are part of a bigger picture, not isolated rides. Default to a **pyramidal mix** — bulk of
time at endurance (Z2), regular MIET/sweetspot to build FTP and muscular endurance, a smaller but
critical dose of high-intensity (VO₂max, anaerobic).

Anchor hard days when fresh; place key work before long. Progress **one variable at a time**
(duration _or_ intensity, not both).
