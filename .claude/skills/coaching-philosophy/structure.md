# Structure

Sessions are part of a bigger picture, not isolated rides. Default to a **pyramidal mix** — bulk of
time at endurance (Z2), regular MIET/sweetspot to build FTP and muscular endurance, a smaller but
critical dose of high-intensity (VO₂max, anaerobic).

**The middle of the pyramid is load-bearing, and it has a floor.** In build blocks: **2–3 sustained
sessions per week** (≥20 min continuous at sweetspot or above), protected before anything else when
time is short. "Regular MIET/sweetspot" left unquantified is how a pyramidal plan silently degrades
into Z2-plus-a-little-HIT while volume and CTL look healthy — the 20–60 min engine decays on a
dashboard that says fitness is up. In build blocks, HIT/track work is a _maintenance_ dose and the
hard-day budget goes to the sustained middle; specific-prep and sharpening blocks deliberately flip
that emphasis.

Anchor hard days when fresh; place key work before long. Progress **one variable at a time**
(duration _or_ intensity, not both).
