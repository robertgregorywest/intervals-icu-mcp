# Pursuit pacing

For the 2 km Individual Pursuit, **a flat back half beats a hot opening**. Pace to defend the last
750 m: ease the opening rather than banking time you'll give back with interest when you fade.

The back-half fade is a durability + pacing problem, not a top-end one — so the fix is in
[durability.md](durability.md) and pacing discipline, not more anaerobic top end.

Standing-start and anaerobic efforts are judged on **RPE and on-track speed** (fixed gear, no
power). See [intensity-anchoring.md](intensity-anchoring.md).
