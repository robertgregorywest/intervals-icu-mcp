# Test cadence

When and how to retest.

- **MAP ramp** every 6–8 weeks or at mesocycle starts. The activity-naming mechanics that make MAP
  surface in `get_coaching_context` (`MAP ramp test` prefix, `(skip)` to exclude a botched one) live
  in the `intervals-coach` skill.
- **FTP/threshold benchmark** early in each build and again mid-build. (Which metric is the season's
  _priority_ is season-scoped — see `docs/personal/season.md`.)
- **Durability check** — fresh vs. late-ride sweetspot (after 2–3 h) every block or two.
- **On-track** — 2 km IP time-trial and schedule-paced efforts to track pacing and the standing
  start.

## Principles

- **Peaks are attempt-limited.** A power curve only reflects durations recently attempted at full
  gas. Without a recent maximal attempt at ~20 min or longer, the curve says nothing about the
  engine there — an apparent "no decline" may just be "no attempt". Schedule tests or races that
  force real attempts at the durations that matter; never read fitness off a curve nobody has tried
  to move.
- **Races as field benchmarks.** An open TT is a calibrated, maximally-motivated ~20-min test —
  with same-course history, the longest-baseline benchmark available. A few per season benchmark
  both the engine and the ability to deliver it in position. A gap between tested power and raced
  power is itself a diagnostic signal (position/delivery), distinct from engine decline — treat the
  two independently before re-anchoring zones off a race.
