# Test cadence

When and how to retest.

- **MAP ramp** every 6–8 weeks or at mesocycle starts. For `get_coaching_context` to surface MAP,
  name the activity with the prefix `MAP ramp test`. Exclude a botched test by renaming it to
  contain `(skip)`.
- **FTP/threshold benchmark** early in each build and again mid-build. (Which metric is the season's
  _priority_ is season-scoped — see `docs/personal/season.md`.)
- **Durability check** — fresh vs. late-ride sweetspot (after 2–3 h) every block or two.
- **On-track** — 2 km IP time-trial and schedule-paced efforts to track pacing and the standing
  start.
