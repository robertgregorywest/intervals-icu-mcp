Microintervals: 30s on / 30s off keeps cumulative time at high aerobic strain while limiting peripheral fatigue per rep. Useful when 4×4 has been overdone or when targeting repeatability rather than peak elicitation.

- Warm-up 15m 50-60%

16x
  - On 30s 110%
  - Off 30s 50%

- Cooldown 10m 45%
