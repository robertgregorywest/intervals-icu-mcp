seedId: vo2-preloaded-shorts
name: VO2 preloaded shorts
folder: "Coach: VO2 Max"
basis: MAP
purpose: Advanced VO2 session — only once 30/30s are well tolerated, and never as the first VO2 session of a block.
---

Each series opens with a ~2-min 'preload' interval to drive VO2 upward fast, then a short 30s recovery preserves the elevated state, so the short 30/15s reps that follow start already near 90% VO2peak instead of climbing into it. Net effect: more time at high fraction of VO2max per session than plain 30/30s. Use when 30/30s have been well tolerated and you want to extract more aerobic stimulus per session. Sources: Odden et al. 2024 (HIT time-at-fraction-of-VO2max); Vaccari et al. 2020 (VO2 kinetics, short vs long).

- Warm-up 15m 50-60%

Series 3x
  - Preload 2m 95-100% 95rpm
  - Short rec 30s 50%
  12x
    - On 30s 100-105%
    - Off 15s 50%
  - Series rec 3m 50%

- Cooldown 10m 45%
