Classic VO₂max session. 4-minute hard with equal recovery elicits the highest fraction of time at VO₂max for a given session length (Seiler et al., 2013). Aim to hold final rep ≥ first rep power.

- Warm-up 15m 50-60%

4x
  - On 4m 95-102%
  - Off 4m 50%

- Cooldown 10m 45%
