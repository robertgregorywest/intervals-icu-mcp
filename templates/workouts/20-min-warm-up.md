Standardised pre-race warm-up: a graded build through endurance to a minute at threshold, then three 10-second activations and a final 5-second effort with full recoveries between.

- 5m 50-60%
- 3m 60-70%
- 1m 75-85%
- 1m 95-105%
- Recovery 2m 45-55%
- Active 0m10s 150-200%
- Recovery 1m 45-55%
- Active 0m10s 150-200%
- Recovery 2m 45-55%
- Active 0m10s 150-200%
- Recovery 2m 45-55%
- Active 0m5s 150-200%
- Cooldown 10m 40-50%
